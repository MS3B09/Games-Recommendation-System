## Stack

Netlify frontend and FastAPI Cloud Hobby backend. No accounts. Reuse the actual installed stack; older proposed router/chart packages are not installed merely because an early plan named them.

## Users
Gamers looking for their next game; recruiters and clients assessing full-stack and AI/ML engineering.

## Product Purpose
Help players find games through favorite titles, explicit constraints and feedback, with an independent conversational assistant. Add catalog exploration through the original dashboard's analytics in a clearer design. Demonstrate full-stack and AI/ML engineering with honest evidence in an immediately accessible guest experience.
