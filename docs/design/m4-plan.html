What I would build.

One integrated extension of Current, not a separate chatbot page. The first release uses the existing catalog; catalog expansion belongs to M5. The content above illustrates placement and state handling, not measured recommendation quality.

The request

A labeled, optional text field below favorite chips. Keep the current multi-choice controls, price slider, exclusion undo and primary action. The explicit controls win over inferred intent.

  • Favorite-only and text-only discovery both remain possible.
  • Interpret clear limits into visible criteria; surface conflicting interpretations before applying changes.
  • Keep hard constraints distinct from soft preferences and unresolved requests.

The shortlist

Applied criteria and honest uncertainty appear above the existing genre-led game rows. Evidence and tradeoffs stay close to the recommendations.

  • Combine seed, query-vector and lexical signals with bounded feedback.
  • Verify supported filters after retrieval; never silently relax them.
  • Validate generated game IDs, facts and evidence; replace invalid explanations with templates.

The conversation

A compact refinement area follows the shortlist. It carries the active criteria forward, shows what changed, and lets the player start again.

  • Retain at most 20 recent messages and normalized active criteria in session storage.
  • Reload restores the tab’s conversation; local history does not become a permanent chat archive.
  • Clear my saved data also clears the conversation and invalidates in-flight responses.

Free, bounded AI

Groq is an optional enhancement for interpretation and explanations. Core ranking remains useful when it is disabled, busy, unavailable or out of quota.

  • At most one interpretation and one batched explanation call per request, with time and token bounds.
  • Atomic database-backed global budget reservations survive server restarts; local throttling bounds client traffic.
  • A server-only Groq key and configured free-tier model are needed for live AI checks. Secrets are configured locally, never pasted into chat.

Acceptance evidence

Check the Hades scenario, multi-turn criteria retention, contradictions, reload, clearing, unsupported claims, malformed provider output, timeout and exhausted budgets.

  • Exercise AI-off behavior and hard constraints end to end.
  • Check keyboard use, mobile/desktop layout, accessibility and existing guest behavior.
  • Record measured results and remaining limitations, then stop at the M4 milestone gate.
